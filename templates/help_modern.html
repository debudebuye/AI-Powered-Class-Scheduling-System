Getting Started

New to the system? Follow these steps to get started:

  1. Login as admin
  2. Add departments, courses, and instructors
  3. Configure rooms and time slots
  4. Generate your first timetable

Generate Timetable

To generate an AI-optimized timetable:

  1. Go to Dashboard
  2. Click "Generate Timetable"
  3. Select department and batch
  4. Let AI create the optimal schedule

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Genetic Algorithm?

The genetic algorithm is an AI technique that mimics natural evolution to find optimal solutions. It creates multiple schedule variations and evolves them to find the best one.

How long does generation take?

Typically 10-30 seconds depending on the complexity. The AI evaluates thousands of possible schedules to find the optimal solution.

Can I edit generated timetables?

Yes! After generation, you can manually edit any class assignment. The system will warn you about conflicts if they arise.

How do I export timetables?

Click the "Export PDF" button on any generated timetable. You can download and print professional-looking schedules.
